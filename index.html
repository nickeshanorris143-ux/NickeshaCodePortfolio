03. Limit the Limitations

Project Description

Limit the Limitations is a semester project exploring the challenges and restrictions faced by international students pursuing higher education in the United States. The website examines the human impact of immigration policies, visa restrictions, SEVIS records, and practical training opportunities. It also highlights the broader effects on universities and the American economy. The goal is to encourage awareness, reflection, and conversations about educational opportunities for international students.

Technical Information

HTML5 for content and website structure, CSS3 for layout and responsive styling, and JavaScript for interactive buttons, navigation highlighting, and visitor feedback.
